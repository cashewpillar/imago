How this is computed & what to keep in mind

  • Analytical, not actual-credited: each line is balance × stated rate, net of a flat 20% withholding-tax assumption — not a reconstruction of amounts actually credited to the account.
  • Manual tracking dependency: balances and rates are only as accurate and current as your last manual update in this app; gaps or lag in data entry directly skew the computed historical %.
  • Sampled monthly: each point is the account data as of month-end (or today, for the current month) — balance history itself is only recorded when a balance actually changes during a save, not a guaranteed daily snapshot, so a month's sample can reflect a stale value if nothing was logged that month.
  • Assumed constant where unrecorded: if an account has never had a rate change logged (common for accounts created before rate history existed, or never rate-edited since), its current rate is assumed to have applied since it was opened, rather than showing no data — real historical rate changes before you started logging them aren't captured.
  • TD rollovers aren't linked: closing a time deposit and opening its replacement are unconnected records; the old account's contribution simply stops at its closure date and the new one picks up — any delay between closing and logging the new account shows as a dip.
  • Flat 20% tax assumption: applied uniformly to all accounts regardless of any account-specific tax treatment.
  • Benchmarks are shown as year-over-year % change: PH Inflation and PHP Depreciation are plotted as their YoY % change (not the raw CPI index level or the raw ₱/$ rate), so they sit on the same % scale as net yield and can be compared directly — but the underlying absolute numbers (today's actual exchange rate, the index level) aren't shown here.
  • Benchmark data is a frozen snapshot: USD/PHP and PH CPI were fetched once and bundled into the app (see scripts/benchmarks-data.js) — they won't pick up new months automatically. PH CPI's underlying index only starts in 2010, so its YoY line can't begin until 2011.
