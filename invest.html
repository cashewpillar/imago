Fees & taxes

What these numbers already account for, what they don't yet, and one thing worth flagging as genuinely uncertain rather than guessing at it.

Already reflected in the numbers
  • TER — deducted from the fund's NAV daily by the fund itself. Already baked into every price point used here; a separate "TER drag" line would double-count it.
  • Dividend withholding tax — these are Ireland-domiciled "Acc" share classes: dividends are reinvested internally, net of source-country withholding tax (reduced under the US-Ireland treaty vs. a US-domiciled fund). Already baked into the price.
Trackable now
  • Broker fee on buy — optional field on Add ticker / Add investment. Adds to cost basis (what you truly paid) without earning any growth, since a fee never buys fund units.
  • Real PHP cost per buy — enter the PHP you actually paid to convert (ground truth, since your broker's real rate and spread differ from the bundled reference series) or, if you converted PHP→USD before the buy itself, a conversion date to estimate from instead. Shown per-entry in the history list and totalled on the Total Invested tile.
Not tracked yet (dev TODO)
  • Sell-side commission — no SELL tracking exists in this app at all yet, buy-only.
  • FX conversion spread when funding the account (PHP→USD) — different from the reference USD/PHP rate shown elsewhere here.
  • Bid-ask spread on execution.
IBKR tiered pricing, for reference (verify against your own account — schedules change): roughly 0.05% of trade value, minimum ≈£1/$1.70, plus a small exchange fee (≈0.0045%, min £0.10) and clearing fee (≈£0.06) — applied the same way to both buy and sell, not a free-buy/paid-sell asymmetry. This is IBKR-specific; a different broker needs its own numbers here.
Philippine tax — not tax advice
PH capital gains tax (NIRC) specifically targets shares of domestic PH corporations and real property — foreign-listed securities like these generally fall outside that regime. Whether frequent trading could get characterized by the BIR as "engaged in the business of buying/selling securities" (taxed as regular income) rather than passive investing is a genuine gray area that depends on individual facts. This app doesn't compute or assert a tax position — talk to a PH tax professional about your specific situation.
